Repair Help

Symptoms that usually need diagnosis

TV has sound but no picture, or the screen looks very dim. This can point to backlight, panel, power or board-related faults.

Display & Backlight
Common Repair Scenario

TV does not turn on, keeps restarting, or remains stuck on the logo. Power supply or main-board diagnosis may be required.

Power & Startup
Common Repair Scenario

HDMI, USB, Wi-Fi or app functions stop working while the TV otherwise powers on. Connectivity or software checks may help.

Connectivity
Common Repair Scenario

Lines, patches, flicker or partial picture can have several causes, so the screen and connected boards should be inspected first.

Picture Faults
Common Repair Scenario

Smart TV apps may freeze or fail after updates. Software reset, storage checks or hardware diagnosis can be the next step.
