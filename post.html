TV has sound but no picture? Causes, checks and repair options

Saraswati Electronics
TV Repair & Electronics Support
Updated 2026 5 min read TV repair guide

If your TV has sound but the screen is black, very dim, flickering or only visible when a flashlight is pointed at it, the fault can be related to the backlight system. But similar symptoms can also come from the power board, main board, display connections or the panel itself.

The safest approach is to start with a few simple checks and avoid opening the TV unless you are trained to work around high-voltage electronics.

1. Start with the simple checks

Confirm the TV is receiving power, increase brightness, switch inputs, disconnect external HDMI devices, and restart the TV. If possible, try opening the TV menu. If menus are also invisible, the problem is more likely inside the TV than in the connected source device.

A black screen with normal sound does not automatically mean the panel is damaged. Backlight, power and board faults can create very similar symptoms.

2. Check whether the screen is actually producing a faint image

In a dark room, shine a phone flashlight at an angle on the screen while the TV is playing. If you can see a very faint moving image, the LCD panel may still be generating picture data while the backlight system is not illuminating it correctly.

3. Common fault areas a technician may inspect

  • LED backlight strips — failed LEDs can make the whole screen appear dark.
  • Backlight driver or power board — the TV may not supply the correct voltage to the LEDs.
  • Main board — signal, startup or control faults can stop picture output.
  • T-Con / display connections — picture data may not reach the panel correctly on applicable models.
  • Panel — cracks, internal shorts, lines or image-processing faults can involve the display panel itself.

4. Information to share before booking a repair

Send the TV brand, exact model number, screen size, what the standby light does, whether sound works, and a short photo or video of the symptom. For Sony spare-parts enquiries, the model and board/part number are especially useful for compatibility checks.

Do not attempt internal TV repair unless you are qualified. Power-supply components can retain dangerous voltage even after the TV is unplugged.
